Final Fantasy XV

Get ready to be at the center of the ultimate fantasy adventure. Join Crown Prince Noctis and his three loyal companions—Gladiolus, Ignis, and Prompto—on an unforgettable road trip across the breathtaking open world of Eos to reclaim his fallen homeland from the Niflheim Empire. Master an action-packed, real-time combat system that allows you to channel ancestral power to warp effortlessly through the air, cast destructive elemental magic, and execute powerful team-based combo attacks. Between high-stakes battles against larger-than-life mythical beasts, immerse yourself in the journey by driving the customizable Regalia, cooking attribute-boosting meals at campsites, and enjoying side activities like fishing and photography.
