Resident Evil 5

The Umbrella Corporation has been dismantled, but its lethal biological legacy continues to threaten humanity. Step into the boots of seasoned BSAA agent Chris Redfield as he is deployed to the scorching plains of Kijuju, Africa, to investigate a mysterious new outbreak transforming the local populace into aggressive, terrifying creatures known as the Majini. Joined by local BSAA operative Sheva Alomar, you must survive an unprecedented wave of fear where threats lurk just as heavily in broad daylight as they do in the shadows. Built from the ground up for revolutionary co-op gameplay, players must meticulously manage a shared 9-slot real-time inventory, swap weapons on the fly, and execute devastating tag-team melee attacks to uncover the dark truth behind the newly engineered Uroboros virus.
