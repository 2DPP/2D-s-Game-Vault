Resident Evil 6

Experience survival horror on a truly global scale. Blending high-octane action with dramatic horror, Resident Evil 6 spreads a terrifying biological crisis across North America, Eastern Europe, and China as a highly virulent new pathogen—the C-Virus—threatens to plunge the world into mutation-fueled chaos. The narrative unfolds through four separate but seamlessly interwoven campaigns, following franchise favorites Leon S. Kennedy, Chris Redfield, and Ada Wong, alongside newcomer Jake Muller. Take advantage of an evolved, highly dynamic control system that allows you to slide, roll in any direction, take cover, and shoot while moving. Team up online using the innovative Crossover mechanic, which dynamically bridges up to four players together at key narrative intersections, or try your luck hunting down other players as a mutated monster in the unique Agent Hunt mode.
